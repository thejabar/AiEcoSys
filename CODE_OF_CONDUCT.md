# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the AiEcoSys project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good faith and focus on technical issues.
- Provide actionable feedback.
- Protect private information and credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, secrets, or unsafe instructions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private security/contact channel
when available. For sensitive reports, avoid public issue details.
